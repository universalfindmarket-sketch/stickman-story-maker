✨ Stickman Story Maker

Build a story video in steps · personal prototype · no account or paid API

1. Choose your video format

Choose whether you're making a short clip or a longer story.

Your choice helps guide pacing. The final length is selected in the animation step.

2. Choose a stickman world

Pick one visual template. Your scenes will keep this style.

3. Create your three characters

One main character and two side characters. The app draws simple colorful stickmen from your choices.

Character descriptions help keep the same character consistent across scenes. This prototype draws simple stickman designs from your selections; it doesn't call an external image-generation service.

4. Set the animation length

Choose the total target duration for your video.

5. Keep every scene consistent

Add the details that every scene should follow. These guide the scene descriptions generated from your script.

The app applies these notes to its scene cards. Because this is a free local prototype, it does not use a paid AI model to invent detailed scenes automatically.

6. Write your script

Paste your script or write it here. We'll split it into scenes and fit the timing to your chosen duration.
